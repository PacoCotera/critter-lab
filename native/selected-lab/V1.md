# Beecho Lab: playable round 01

This standalone prototype connects one complete local loop: explore, retain a
haul, research a sample, choose a supported genome, incubate, reveal and visit
the same Beecho in its habitat. It uses native C state and rendering; the browser
transports the depicted directional, workspace, Confirm and Back buttons and displays native
frames. Screen illustrations are not clickable controls.

## Play

Home opens on a lab-wide status overview. Up/Down previews Explore, Research,
Incubator or Habitat in the large panel; Confirm enters and Back restores the
same Home selection. The preview reads current state without starting anything.
Research shows retained samples and discoveries, because V1 studies resolve
immediately after the existing explicit cost review/commitment.

Press and release Up/Down to choose a row; Confirm acts. Left and Back return
to the parent screen. Right opens supported read-only detail, never a costly
action or reveal. Research, Critters, Library and Habitat keys switch workspaces
without stopping activities or spending resources. Library contains recorded
findings; Critters contains revealed residents. Start an expedition from Explore, then visit Cargo to
return with your haul. A completed 60-second survey also brings home a sample.
An early return keeps gathered resources but awards no sample. You can discard
a completed resource pack manually. There are no resource filters.

Research the five topics on a sample. Selecting an unknown topic opens a cost
and available-stock review; Start research requires a fresh Confirm. Discarding
a pack likewise has an explicit review and a Keep choice. Discovered topics can be inspected again
without spending. After all five discoveries, Prepare incubation offers two
complete supported Pip configurations: plain coat carrying pale markings, or
expressed pale markings. Another expedition supplies the remaining materials.
Incubation takes 20 active seconds; opening it is deliberate. Visit the habitat,
spend time with your resident, and explore again.

Supplies are whole items, interchangeable only within their own class. The
internal encoding uses100 per item; new Lab and carried inventory must be
multiples100. One pack is10 items and capacity is40 items. Preparation toward
a gathering attempt is separate time, never a fractional item, cargo slot or
transfer. Study costs remain4 or5 items; incubation costs5 of each.

The time/chance playtest fixture schedules an opportunity after4000ms of qualified
preparation per resource class. Each opportunity has a provisional3-of-4 award
chance and yields either one whole item or nothing. Its random state, preparation
and actual outcome are persisted atomically. The screen shows one Next attempt
track from the earliest actual remaining time; historical phases may differ, so
the upcoming class caption remains truthful. Capacity is checked before advancing
an opportunity or drawing chance, preserving progress without losing awards.
No offline catch-up, retry reroll or guaranteed next item is implied. These are
provisional test settings, not canonical balance or measured human engagement.

Sending stops collection and seals the returning haul. Fresh Lab acceptance
ends that expedition, even before its duration threshold; it grants no late sample
or award. The matching receipt closes transport metadata, then a new route starts
a new expedition identity. Cancelling an unsealed send review retains the outing. Preparation stays Companion-side through transfer. A completed
route with no transferable items and no shelf room can finish without a phantom
haul. The three routes still share gathering fixtures; authored distinct events,
risks and drops remain incomplete. Legacy recovery is defined in
[architecture](../../specs/architecture.md#three-device-host-simulator).

The intended Research hierarchy has a collection-wide Overview separate from
each selected sample's overview and workbench. This V1 navigation still enters
the sample list directly; the menu/workbench redesign remains a later slice.

## Boundaries

One local world, eight sample records and eight resident slots. No cloud account,
real sensors, printing, charging, device-to-device transfer or physical display
performance is claimed. Closing or suspending play does not add offline time.
Care is gentle and has no neglect penalty. This is a playable Pip slice, not
the full catalogue, capture/training system or habitat ecology simulation.

The genotype remains engine data. Player screens explain form and expression.
Each individual retains its source sample, founder origin, complete supported
Pip genome, expression and content/art versions. Creation and reveal are separate
durable operations. Gemini source and extraction provenance are in
`design/v1-pip/manifest.json`; the two provisional Pip sprites are playtest art.

## Running and verification

Build `native/selected-lab` with the established CMake toolchain, then run
`selected_lab serve`. Set `BEECHO_V1_SAVE` to an absolute writable save path;
otherwise the program uses a separate `.beecho-v1` save next to its legacy path.
Existing legacy saves are not migrated or overwritten. The existing presenter
serves this executable. `ctest` exercises native input and domain behavior;
`native/tests/test_selected_presenter.py BINARY` checks the HTTP adapter;
`native/tests/test_v1_journey.py BINARY FRAME_DIRECTORY` plays a complete
real-time journey and captures native screens, including suspend and restart.

The rollback checkpoint for this round is retained separately. This document
describes the prototype boundary; test execution and review acceptance must be
reported against the actual delivered revision, not inferred from this plan.

## Pinned genetic content

`pip-proof-v1` is the narrow Pip content package, not a generic genome generator.
Its five variable-locus facts are Crown, Eye rings, Markings, Movement and
Efficiency. The nine baseline module references and three fixed-locus references
remain the immutable Pip baseline specified by `prototype/genetics/content.mjs`;
these are package facts, not five new loci or randomly selected traits. The
five-bit native study mask is not the general engine's 17-reference completeness
mask. `pip-rules-v1` evaluates the fixed adult, healthy/rested, firm-ground,
mild-environment reference context. Future environmental expression must vary
state, not mutate the stored genome or change the identity of an existing resident.

## Lab platform and controls

The Lab reference is Raspberry Pi4 Model B with the 1024×600 display and the
[accepted family control layout](../../design/lab-controls/combined-family-materials.png).
Current executable evidence is native C17 compiled with GCC/CMake/Ninja for Linux
x86-64 on the existing VM and CI. This is not an ESP-IDF Lab application or an
emulated Raspberry Pi. ARM compilation, physical display/input drivers and Pi4
performance remain unverified. ESP-IDF builds the separate Companion scaffold.
