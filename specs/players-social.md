# Players, shared equipment and social play

## Accepted rules

One household kit may serve multiple players. Local kit profiles keep collections, inventory, discoveries and background work separate; core play works standalone from the box, including nearby-kit interaction. Exact local identity, authentication, backup and handover mechanisms remain open. Virtual Lab chips, upgrades and equipped configuration follow the player, not the terminal; physical hardware still constrains supported capabilities.

A shared combined Companion assigns each Probe-mode expedition to one player. Cargo/progress stays attributed to that player when another player uses the device later. Companion use is also switchable between players, preserving each player's critters, training and progress. Physical sharing is not specimen transfer. Companion capacity and detailed offline handover remain open.

Profile selection, physical registration, current operator and expedition ownership are different concepts. Ordinary switching must not be treated as registration reassignment. Companion/Lab tapping and NFC cards are candidate access interactions, not selected hardware or credentials. Recognizing a profile does not itself authorize actions.

Discoveries are personal. Global first-discoverer rankings/rewards are excluded from V1. Encountering a critter may add an encyclopedia entry without transferring it. Individual identity differs from family, genome equivalence, phenotype equivalence and appearance; matching genomes/art never merge records or owners.

## Optional global services

Cloud Pass is optional and adds global trading and breeding, lineage, certificates and minigames. Nearby-kit interaction belongs to the standalone core and must not require this service. Exact nearby transfer/consent, local/global enrollment and reconciliation remain OPEN; no prices are defined. Local creation or possession is not automatic global certification, and optional service access is not a prerequisite for local collections or play.

## V1 social research references

Taking a reference requires explicit specimen-owner agreement naming the recipient and shared study scope. References are recipient-bound and let the recipient study specifically shared properties/clues using their own materials. They carry no donor alleles, founder material, breeding input or generation weights; a clue does not expand the creation candidate set or guarantee the donor's traits.

Collection does not damage, deplete or alter the source. Copies create no tradeable generation stock or repeat supply/points. Consent does not authorize recurring collection, private-genome export, breeding or ownership transfer. An independently created founder retains its true origin, not the researched specimen as a fictitious parent. Findings, effort, consent presentation and limits on new references remain open.

## Proposed rights model

| Concept | Distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Player/account | Enduring identity and private records; shared/family accounts and recovery need design |
| Owner | Recognized ownership rights for an individual; a single owner is proposed, not a co-ownership decision |
| Holder/custodian | Possession plus only specifically granted care rights; not ownership |
| Breeding grant | Scoped permitted use of genetics, distinct from care and custody |
| Public view | Explicitly disclosed facts, never credentials or implicit mutation permission |

Loans, gifts, trades, fees and offspring allocation remain proposed. Any breeding commitment needs agreed offspring ownership and valid consent. Local and nearby-kit actions require valid rights under mechanisms still to be defined. For Cloud Pass operations, proposed permission checking occurs at global acceptance; late receipt of an already accepted result must not duplicate or undo it. Expiry/revocation/reservation and offline intent rules remain open. Grant expiry does not by itself delete a specimen or valid history.

Private knowledge/publication boundaries require explicit permitted fields. A public trait catalogue is not a publication of every player's hidden alleles or research. Scan rewards are separate from discovery bookkeeping; screenshots and physical scans do not prove equivalent real-world travel. See [gameplay](gameplay.md).

## Care and recovery

Care, development, training and bonding are intended experiences. Lifespan, irreversible death, absence penalties and responsibility during borrowing are not settled. Forgiving care is a design goal; its concrete absence rules are not implemented. Account deletion and fictional death are separate operations.

Hardware reset or sale must not implicitly transfer specimens. Core recovery must work from retained local records without requiring Cloud Pass; backups and recovery after device loss remain open. Optional cloud recovery restores only accepted global records available there; it cannot reconstruct local records never received. Registration reassignment and cache removal need explicit privacy/recovery policies. See [cloud synchronization](cloud-sync.md).
