# Players, shared equipment and social play

## Accepted rules

One household kit may serve multiple players. Shared Lab cloud profiles keep collections, inventory, discoveries and background work separate. Virtual Lab chips, upgrades and equipped configuration follow the player, not the terminal; physical hardware still constrains supported capabilities.

A shared Probe assigns each expedition to one player. Cargo/progress stays attributed to that player when another player uses the device later. Companion use is also switchable between players, preserving each player's critters, training and progress. Physical sharing is not specimen transfer. Companion capacity and detailed offline handover remain open.

Profile selection, physical registration, current operator and expedition ownership are different concepts. Ordinary switching must not be treated as registration reassignment. Probe/Companion tapping and NFC cards are candidate access interactions, not selected hardware or credentials. Recognizing a profile does not itself authorize actions.

Discoveries are personal. Global first-discoverer rankings/rewards are excluded from V1. Encountering a critter may add an encyclopedia entry without transferring it. Individual identity differs from family, genome equivalence, phenotype equivalence and appearance; matching genomes/art never merge records or owners.

## V1 social research references

Taking a reference requires explicit specimen-owner agreement naming the recipient and shared study scope. References are recipient-bound and let the recipient study specifically shared properties/clues using their own materials. They carry no donor alleles, founder material, breeding input or generation weights; a clue does not expand the creation candidate set or guarantee the donor's traits.

Collection does not damage, deplete or alter the source. Copies create no tradeable generation stock or repeat supply/points. Consent does not authorize recurring collection, private-genome export, breeding or ownership transfer. An independently created founder retains its true origin, not the researched specimen as a fictitious parent. Findings, effort, consent presentation and limits on new references remain open.

## Proposed rights model

| Concept | Distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Player/account | Enduring identity and private records; shared/family accounts and recovery need design |
| Owner | Recognized ownership rights for an individual; a single owner is proposed, not a co-ownership decision |
| Holder/custodian | Possession plus only specifically granted care rights; not ownership |
| Breeding grant | Scoped permitted use of genetics, distinct from care and custody |
| Public view | Explicitly disclosed facts, never credentials or implicit mutation permission |

Loans, gifts, trades, fees and offspring allocation remain proposed. Any breeding commitment needs agreed offspring ownership and valid consent. Proposed permission checking occurs at cloud acceptance; late receipt of an already accepted result must not duplicate or undo it. Expiry/revocation/reservation and offline intent rules remain open. Grant expiry does not by itself delete a specimen or valid history.

Private knowledge/publication boundaries require explicit permitted fields. A public trait catalogue is not a publication of every player's hidden alleles or research. Scan rewards are separate from discovery bookkeeping; screenshots and physical scans do not prove equivalent real-world travel. See [gameplay](gameplay.md).

## Care and recovery

Care, development, training and bonding are intended experiences. Lifespan, irreversible death, absence penalties and responsibility during borrowing are not settled. Forgiving absence is a proposal, not an implemented lifecycle rule. Account deletion and fictional death are separate operations.

Hardware reset or sale must not implicitly transfer specimens. Cloud can restore accepted records after valid recovery; unsynced activity may be lost. Registration reassignment and cache removal need explicit privacy/recovery policies. See [cloud synchronization](cloud-sync.md).
