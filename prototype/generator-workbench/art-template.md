# Genome-first art template

The [versioned template](art-template.json) presents verified expression as a
positive drawing brief. The owner's [form and skin example](evidence/owner-semantic-surface/README.md)
establishes the active correction: diagnostic envelopes and covering glyphs
communicate construction and material meaning; they do not prescribe every
finished contour or turn skin texture into attached objects.

## Renderer and workbench responsibilities

The workbench validates the source, independently re-evaluates expression and
projects its actual body, appendages, face, surface and pigment owners. Missing
semantic construction remains inspectable upstream. The artist finishes the
supplied form with coherent volume, material treatment and deliberate pixel
craft. Local curvature and optical relief are depiction choices; new organs,
colour domains or inherited characteristics are explicit redesign proposals.

| Subject meaning | Drawing handoff | Inspection retains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Continuous exterior where modeled, regional proportions, taper and waist relationships | Stations, exact coordinates, connected topology and source profile |
| Appendages | Actual role/count/root groups and available chord/span/thickness/taper or connected sections/contact ends | Node dimensions, edges, joints and unresolved physical depth |
| Skin and covering | Material kind, field, coarse/fine element character, flow, exclusions and pigment continuity | Sample counts, contours, root coordinates and construction profiles |
| Colour and marks | Named expressed colours/hex values with their actual body or appendage owners; marks distinct from material light | Allele resolution, masks, marking records and causal trace |

For the retained six-fin subject, describe fins as fins and scales as an
overlapping texture belonging to skin. The pictured triangular footprints and
outlined scale samples are aids to those meanings. This does not imply fins
are hands, horns or feet, or that thin surfaces have measured physical tissue
depth. A graph without a continuous exterior is not silently joined into one.
Sparse fur/feather coverage retains its field, flow, contour effect and visible
gaps; semantic prose must not replace it with a full coat.

Current body pigmentation offers only charcoal/slate and russet. Plain colour
names, hex values and ownership clarify those expressions. Broader inherited
colour variety requires a separate versioned pigment-content proposal; a style
instruction is not the source of a new palette.

## Pixel craft and acceptance

Describe technique directly: deliberate contiguous pixel clusters, controlled
stepped contours, coherent form-following shadow/highlight groups and selective
crisp material edges. Frame the complete connected subject with readable
appendages and let existing eyes carry character focus. The supplied pigment
domains remain recognizable through material light and shade.

The [C18 reference](../../design/game-art-proposals/35-vault-composition/18-c-refined.png)
supplies interface/contour craft and optical density; it does not supply a pet's
skeleton or establish a complete character style. The owner-liked
[pet family](evidence/art-reset/README.md) and
[Gemini transformations](evidence/owner-pet-transformations/README.md) provide
character direction. A reference is a provider input only when actually attached.

Assess three distinct questions on actual returned pixels: recognizable source
meaning, coherent appealing pet portrayal, and intentional pixel craft at the
intended display size. A pleasing smooth gradient is not a HiBit craft pass.
Source/prompt tests do not approve an illustration, rig, animation or biological
or hardware capability. Retain exact submitted text, attached inputs, original
output bytes/hashes, observed provider state and the findings beside the result.

## Preserved factual authority and versions

The current presentation versions are template4 and module-scene-art/2.
They change renderer prose, not allele resolution, the diagnostic geometry or
genome/expression identities. Historical stored prompts and artifacts remain
unchanged. Replaying a source may produce the current presentation; it does not
promise byte-identical historical prompt text or a reproduced image.

Complete named bindings remain in the exported packet: identity, context,
anatomy, proportions, surfaces, movement, limitations and causal trace. Exact
sampling counts, coordinates, shapes and dictionaries remain available there.
The concise prompt preserves meaningful roles, proportions, pigment and material
fields without presenting the JSON audit as instructions to the image model.
Contradiction diagnosis and source repair belong to the workbench.

The bounds remain64KiB per subject,8192characters per audit binding and
32768characters per assembled prompt. Oversized projections reject upstream.
The displayed12-hex #G/#E prefixes are lookup fingerprints, not reversible
genome packing or guaranteed uniqueness. Full canonical hashes remain exported;
the separate [tree codec proof](evidence/genome-tree-codec/README.md) supplies
reversible data transport. Neither scanning nor sharing grants ownership or
breeding permission.

## Proposed multi-pass pet production

Art direction, pixel art and genomics define the handoff together. The stages
answer different questions; they do not require four generation calls.

| Stage | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Outline, when useful | Clarify organization, roots and overlap spacing; label the diagram as a construction/material aid |
| Illustration | Finish connected volume, appendage form, material and expressed pigment with deliberate pixel craft |
| Pet treatment | Establish inviting character through actual or explicitly proposed form, face and gesture; may combine with illustration |
| Animation, after pet selection | Explore one defined response loop from a selected master, preserving identity across frames |

**Same-source portrayal** preserves semantic organization, part roles, root
relationships, characteristic proportions, features, pigment and material fields.
**Art-first redesign** may explore changed anatomy, proportions, colours or
markings before coding. Retain ancestry and a short changed-trait list; leave a
resulting expression identity absent until those changes have a resolved source.
The owner's red pet is useful direction for this second route.

One focused correction may address an actual image defect. After two failed
rounds, diagnose framing before a third request. The prior two-pass experiment
is stopped. The subsequent single-source result improves observable orientation
and material fidelity but remains below pet-master acceptance. The planned
body-hierarchy generation was held after the latest owner feedback; semantic
handoff repair precedes another illustration. Animation and game integration
remain dependent on a selected master and their own concrete acceptance gates.
