# Genome-first art template

The [versioned template](art-template.json) supplies a positive renderer brief
for the workbench's current verified creature. Version2 replaces the audit-wall
text after the owner's2October correction. It leads with deliberate Critter Lab
HiBit art direction and follows with a readable description of the resolved
subject. [Four supplied image/prompt comparisons](evidence/renderer-brief/README.md)
show why rendering quality, anatomical fidelity and pet appeal need separate
assessment.

## Renderer and workbench responsibilities

The renderer draws the supplied subject. Its text describes composition,
silhouette, intentional pixel clusters, density, lighting, surface treatment and
presentation, followed by source-derived body organization, rooted appendage
roles/counts, proportions, actual facial features, pigment/covering fields and a
quiet static pose. Organic-looking shading is provisional illustration style;
actual continuous tissue, face and material coverage come from the shared
generator. Developmental stations are distinct from visible segmentation.

The workbench verifies resolved status, content/schema identity and input/result
digests, then independently re-evaluates the shared engine before projecting.
Unresolved, rejected, tampered or oversized inputs fail here. The image renderer
is not asked to diagnose contradictions or repair genes/topology. Model limits,
source traces, ownership/incubation disclaimers and unsupported movement records
belong to this upstream inspection, rather than the drawing instructions.

## Preserved factual authority

The complete named bindings remain in `packet.prompt.bindings`: result identity,
context, anatomy, proportions, surfaces, eligible movement, limitations and
causal trace. Existing source dictionaries and retained coordinates remain
lossless. The adapter creates a separate concise prose description from the same
verified result; it never reinterprets allele copies or chooses a species preset.
Counts, root groups, shape relationships, feature availability, pigment
partitions, covering extent and expression gates survive that description.

The copyable `packet.prompt.text` is the renderer brief, not the JSON audit.
This concise concept prompt is not an exact coordinate transport. Exact marking
positions and geometry remain inspectable in bindings/reference artifacts;
text alone cannot establish pixel-level fidelity. Unknown exterior/mask details
remain source gaps, and the renderer cannot silently complete missing anatomy.

The existing bounds remain64KiB per subject,8192characters per audit binding and
32768characters per assembled prompt. Overflow rejects upstream rather than
silently truncating facts. This is a prototype contract, not a provider claim.

## Style and acceptance
