# Research and creation: choose what to learn, then what to make

**Accepted V1 design; not implemented.** The research structure is accepted: choose an investigation, follow relevant findings, resolve all required information and select a complete supported configuration before creation. Costs, timing, exact tests and the fictional content below remain open or illustrative. See [gameplay](../specs/gameplay.md) for the governing rule and the [creation boundary](../specs/sample-to-critter-contract.md) for proposed technical behavior.

## A short investigation with a consequential ending

V1 uses **two initial studies and, when the findings require it, one follow-up**, followed by choosing a complete supported configuration and deliberately creating it. That is four or five meaningful commitments on the introductory path, excluding acquisition, browsing and Open. It is not a minimum: an earlier supported comparison may answer overlapping questions, and already resolved information needs no repeated study.

Research adapts within authored content; required knowledge does not imply fixed test order. The player chooses which uncertainty to investigate first and, when a finding leaves competing interpretations, which relevant comparison to perform. A follow-up must resolve something the previous result actually left uncertain. Repeating an answered study provides no fresh result or bonus.

For this V1, all configurations in a sample share its required region set. **Every required region must be resolved before final selection.** Choosing an apparently simpler outcome cannot hide or bypass a locked region. Optional deeper comparisons can remain afterward only if they do not conceal required genomic information.

## One worked investigation

**Entire example PROPOSED:** an ordinary field- or console-produced sample has clues suggesting a stable outer structure and an unexplained glow. These fictional clues do not prove real genes. Cosmetic curiosity and possible signaling utility are equally valid goals.

**Material study** examines what structures the sample supports. **Response study** investigates the glow under supplied comparison conditions. No manual tuning or quiz is required; the player chooses an informative question and commits its displayed reagent.

Material-first reveals compatible form alternatives and helps a player decide whether the sample interests them. Response-first reveals that illumination matters, but leaves reflection versus retained emission unresolved. That finding changes the useful next action: run **Compare after illumination** instead of repeating the original study. It resolves the remaining interaction information. A player who knew that comparison already could attempt it earlier with the supported setup; encyclopedia knowledge grants guidance, not permission.

Conceptual bitmap progression for the material-first route—not a device layout or encoding. On a response-first route, structural information remains unresolved until investigated:

| Knowledge state | Region A: structural information | Region B: response information | Region C: interaction/variation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Unresolved | Unresolved | Unresolved |
| Material study | Known alternatives | Unresolved | Unresolved |
| Response study | Known alternatives | Known response | Unresolved explanation |
| Relevant follow-up | Known | Known | Known; carried versus expressed distinguished |

After full unlock, two authored compatible configurations might express different glow patterns while carrying a known unexpressed variation. These are hypothetical content relationships requiring definition, not approved powers. Explain expressed/carried properties and contextual capabilities in plain language, without requiring allele strings. Selecting one records the complete supported genome. Create introduces no hereditary lottery. Artwork must depict the resulting phenotype; the reveal adds presence, not undisclosed genes.

## Variety, progress and purpose

Samples vary in starting clues, supported alternatives, unresolved interactions and supplies—not merely their bitmap decoration. A sample whose interaction information is already established may need only the initial studies. Preserve stable sample identity and recorded results; later complexity never revokes an existing individual's completeness.

Missing reagent pauses the affected study, preserving findings and other available work. Return shows the unresolved question and a collection or console-crafting lead. The encyclopedia retains findings and their conditions. Uncertain submissions resume the same operation rather than spending or resolving again.

The tradeoff is a small set of meaningful investigation rules instead of unlimited experiments. Reusable content rules can be produced through automated authoring tools and validated before use; this does not require hand-authoring each sample or claim an implemented content pipeline. Its appeal is following curiosity, choosing investigations from evidence, and understanding your creation—not optimizing a compulsory “best” critter.
