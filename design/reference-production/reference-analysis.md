## Focused art review

The corrected [`sheet-native.png`](exports/sheet-native.png) and [`overview-offline.png`](exports/overview-offline.png) were inspected at 1× beside C18 and the prior native Overview. The first export's uniformly heavy blue panel contours, flat coarse Essence, overpowering continuous focus rim, fallback monospaced text, crowded Research line and missing sample capsule were returned as one source-level correction. The final export has quieter broken inner frame rules, bracket-led focus, a rounded Essence with upper-left transmission and lower/right depth, loaded type, clear Research text and an authored capsule in its prior role. The Data card and Energy crystal retain distinct silhouettes. Art direction accepts this **offline reconstruction exercise**; exact screenshot pixel equivalence, native runtime fidelity and the broader Lab screen system remain unverified. The retained Overview information and console semantics were not redesigned here.

The final proof received one narrow layout repair after the card-heading ink touched the top inner blue rule. All four heading baselines moved down without shrinking their type; inspection of the updated 1× proof confirms visible clearance above each title and below it before the separator. This check compares rendered glyph ink bounds with authored frame paths, since font metrics and text baselines alone did not expose the collision.
