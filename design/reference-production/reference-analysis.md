## Current composition and review

Overview answers four player questions at once: where the first sample comes from (Explore), what knowledge is retained (Research), whether growth is underway (Incubator) and whether life is present (Habitat). All four answers remain readable. The [current 1024×600 offline proof](exports/overview-offline.png) uses a 24px outer perimeter, approximately 16px major gutters, five-destination navigation and one shared status field with open dividers. These dimensions are implementation choices, not C18 measurements or a quality claim. Each status has a concise factual caption and a large authored topic motif, while Explore carries the next-step cue. No specimen, research result, expedition route, active embryo or resident is depicted.

The owner rejected the earlier PR33 composition because its margins and icon proportions were wrong and four similar framed text cards dominated the screen despite C18-styled edges. **That earlier composition pass is withdrawn.** The rework replaces those boxes with larger, materially distinct compass, empty record, empty chamber and unoccupied habitat-niche motifs. In a focused 1× art review, the first rework draft still left too much empty graphite, with small desaturated subjects and weak Incubator/Habitat identity. The corrected sheet and proof now give all four motifs comparable optical weight, enough saturated craft to avoid a disabled reading, and clear empty-state interiors. Header resources stand alongside the type rather than serving as minor accents; the footer's redundant Explore sentence has moved into the Explore area. C18's study-specific visual subjects are not copied as false Overview facts.

Art direction accepts this corrected **offline Overview composition** as a bounded reference-derived proof. The conclusion rests on native-size balance, subject identity, visible status facts, spacing and material relationships in the actual export, not on its SVG count or dimensions. The selected visual direction still needs owner review; native runtime fidelity and broader Lab screens remain unverified. Text ink clearance was checked against frame paths, including the raised header progress lines. A future native screen must separately prove intended console behavior and actual rendering.
