# Lab ergonomics: two dimensioned comparison targets

28 September 2026. Hardware proposal; case remains open. No ergonomic standard, human test, parts selection or mechanical fit is claimed. See the [comparison preview](deck-comparison.png), [clustered full-scale template](clustered-full-scale.svg) and [spread full-scale template](spread-full-scale.svg).

## Play drives the shape

Treat the Lab as a stable seated desktop instrument, with one-hand serial operation as the baseline and two-handed exploration as an enjoyable option. Left cross plus right Zoom/action cluster is a proposed two-hand posture, not proof of comfort. Either single hand must be able to complete the whole sequence by repositioning; no chords, held modifier, simultaneous input or reflex timing. Avoid requiring Inspect/Zoom before every study, or a success acknowledgement after discovery. The worthwhile ritual is choosing a subject, looking closer when curious, seeing actual terms, deliberately starting, and returning to a retained finding.

Common hand transfers are cross↔Confirm and cross↔Zoom. Workspace changes happen at boundaries. Put frequent controls near each other, with space to press and grip them independently; compare that against more separated two-hand resting zones. Left-only play must allow a front/outer approach to the dial rather than sweeping through Confirm. Keep Cancel LEFT and Confirm RIGHT in both layouts; never reverse that learned order to address handedness.

## Sourced display reference

The selected Waveshare **7inch HDMI LCD (H)** product page embeds this [manufacturer dimensional drawing](https://www.waveshare.com/img/devkit/LCD/7HP/Exterior-Size.jpg). Its back photo explicitly says H, Rev4.1. The maximum depicted outline is **164.90 mm wide × 124.27 mm high**; the depicted front glass is **164.90 × 106.96 mm**. These are not active-image dimensions. Mounting centre spacing is separately shown; HDMI/USB/VGA cables, mounting structure and bend space are not included in our fit claim. The particular revision/order must be verified before mechanical commitment.  The [H wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/7inch_HDMI_LCD_%28H%29) reports about 13.8 mm overall thickness including HDMI; that does not include connected cable clearance. This is manufacturer evidence, not our measurement.

The approximately 165 mm module can sit centrally above either wider deck. That does not make a 280 or 320 mm enclosure necessary: these widths come from control/play hypotheses, not from the screen diagonal. Display position/tilt and all internal packaging remain separate open choices.

All control sizes, positions and sweep circles are **mock-up targets chosen for comparison**, not manufacturer parts or human-factors limits. Distances are calculated centre-to-centre on the flat surface, not arm travel, comfortable reach or a timed efficiency result. The dashed sweep is an arbitrary trial envelope; actual finger/hand sweep must be traced with people gripping a real or representative knob. A has about 20.4 mm between the nominal Confirm and dial cap edges, but only about 10.9 mm to the trial swept envelope; B about 21.8/12.8 mm respectively. Neither gap establishes safe independent use.

Primary cap front-edge clearance is 23 mm in A and 32 mm in B; this does not establish palm support. Table-supported forearms are the trial baseline. The B cross is moved forward of its upper-left workspace grid to prevent overlap, leaving 10 mm to the front; test that placement rather than assume it supports a resting hand.

## Nominal starting size and side profile

Start the comparison at **280 × 125 mm for the control deck**: it preserves the owner-directed hierarchy while reducing the calculated recurring transfers relative to the split layout. Keep **320 × 135 mm** as the spacious comparator; it may offer better distinct resting zones but demands larger single-hand relocations. This is a mock-up starting preference, not a final enclosure or an established comfort result.

Trial surface heights/slopes: front edge 20 mm and 25 mm above table, with independently adjustable 0°, 8° and 12° control-surface angles on **both** layouts so size is not confused with slope. Positive slope is an experiment, not a recommendation for wrist posture. At full depth, A rises another 17.4 mm at 8° or 26.0 mm at 12°; B another 18.8 / 28.1 mm. These are calculated plane rises, not enclosure clearance. Try a rounded front edge without assuming an attached wrist rest is beneficial. Screen angle should be adjusted separately for seated viewing; do not freeze it from control-deck slope.

Printer roll, feed mechanism, tear edge, scanner, compute, connectors and ventilation are unallocated. The front marking reserves access intent only; a low mock-up face cannot establish printable hardware fit. A final integrated Lab may be deeper/taller or have a different paper route after those interfaces are scoped. No nominal overall-case height/depth is defensible yet.

## Guidance and evidence limits

[OSHA computer-workstation evaluation guidance](https://www.osha.gov/etools/computer-workstations/checklists/evaluation) supports relaxed shoulders, elbows near the body, aligned wrists/hands, frequently used controls nearby, stable surfaces and avoiding hard contact edges. It is workstation guidance, not a numeric specification for a game console, children, button diameter or knob spacing. It also suggests horizontal or slightly negative keyboard slope; hence a positive instrument slope must be compared rather than treated as automatically ergonomic. Our play-posture/size recommendations are inferences to test, not OSHA-approved geometry.

## Smallest useful physical comparison

Use the same screen sequence and movable full-size mock controls on A and B. Keep roles/order, screen position, base stability and slope comparable before varying one factor. Run seated two-hand, right-only and left-only: Research→record→feature→optional Inspect/Zoom/pan→Cancel→review→fresh Start→retained discovery→Library→Research. Trace hand movement, look for unintended neighbouring presses, dial grip passing over Confirm, wrist bend, forearm lift, screen occlusion, slipping and paper interference. Ask which exploration gestures feel inviting and which relocations become tedious. Do not score fewer movements as more fun automatically. Inert paper caps can reveal spatial problems; real cap travel, force and knob feel require physical controls. No human observation has been performed.

## Common play sequences and friction

| Player intent | Proposed sequence | Interaction requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose a genome | Research key → cross → Confirm | Open the highlighted record directly; no extra acknowledgement. |
| Look closer | Cross → Inspect → optional Zoom/pan | Keep feature and location visible. Inspection is free and optional; never a compulsory prelude to study. |
| Start research | Confirm opens cost review → fresh Confirm on Start | Topic, cost and stock together; no required Inventory trip or dial ritual. Held input cannot start twice. |
| Enjoy discovery | Result stays with the researched record | No mandatory success-dismissal press. Inspect at leisure; preserve unknown information until research reveals it. |
| Consult Library | Library key → browse → Research key | Restore record, focus and view. Switching workspaces never spends or cancels submitted work. |

Inspect opens detail; Confirm opens/selects the focused supported item and commits only from an explicit Start review. Do not require both buttons for identical detail. Cancel closes detail or leaves an unsubmitted review and restores context; it does not delete anything or abort committed research. Zoom retains its view-scale role, with clear bounds/inactive feedback where irrelevant. It must not secretly become navigation or reveal unknown traits.

Two-hand exploration is the intended longer-session pattern; quick checking and all complete journeys must remain possible serially with either hand. This is a design target, not measured predominant behavior. Test repeated cross/action/zoom cycles, not just a single comfortable pose. The larger spread arrangement's forward cross is only 10 mm from the front edge; the clustered layout has 23 mm ahead of the primary button edges. Neither amount proves palm support. Use table-supported forearms as the initial trial and observe actual contact.
