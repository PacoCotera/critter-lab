# Probe gathering and genomic research: design review

**Proposals for owner discussion, 1 October 2026. Coding paused.** This is one connected design comparison, not a selected mechanic, approved visual encoding, deployment or playtest result. Current product rules remain in [gameplay](../specs/gameplay.md), [genetics](../specs/genetics.md) and [the sample contract](../specs/sample-to-critter-contract.md). Detailed existing evidence is in [the exploration study](expedition-map-study/README.md) and [research and creation](research-and-creation.md).

## The problem both domains must solve

The current field loop acquires whole finite items immediately, but similar stops and road-bound travel make exploration repetitive. The bench retains facts and explains supported forms, but choosing paid studies is not yet an engaging investigation. Removing waits, enlarging art and changing menu labels do not finish either redesign.

The shared player journey should be: **notice something → decide what to investigate → bring home a particular sample and useful supplies → investigate its hereditary possibilities → use a finding to choose what follows → deliberately create one understood individual.** Lab, Companion and Dock retain the same accepted records. An outing for supplies remains useful without another sample. Field preparation waits remain rejected.

## Three connected directions

| Candidate | Companion / Probe | Lab bench | Why repeat? | Greatest weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Follow the clues** | Open reachable ground, distinct visible opportunities and retained leads. Choose a useful supply detour or follow a sample lead; collect disclosed bundles immediately. | Choose a sample-specific question and see both justified reference alternatives together. Findings suggest the next study or resupply. | Different useful contents and locations change route order; different samples change questions. | Better travel may still feed the same pickup itinerary and paid checklist. |
| **B. Investigate hereditary relationships** | Explore for useful supplies and new samples with truthful provenance. A reached subject offers a meaningful observation or collection; its consequence must be authored, not a random reward icon. | Choose an analysis scope that answers a particular uncertainty. Examine sample-specific evidence and its reference explanation; what remains unknown changes the next useful operation. | Different supported relationships change the investigation, rather than just the portrait or ID. | Needs new authored evidence/content. Selecting a scope and receiving a result may still be too passive. |
| **C. Build a collection with purpose** | An intended research/creation goal guides which opportunities to pursue. Away Companion uses retained local information, not imagined live Lab stock. | Compare retained records, discover which sample might support a desired combination, then freely compare fully understood forms and retain a reversible creation draft. | Personal preferences and real collection differences create goals. | Can feel like shopping among predetermined designs rather than scientific discovery. |

These are alternatives, not three implementation streams. Our recommendation is to discuss **B first**, with A's curiosity and C's eventual personal purpose as context. This does not authorize combining their entire scope.

## Worked field decision

Candidate A's provisional example illustrates useful procedural variation: a player wants four Data units for their own research purpose. One outing has two at Camp, a nearby Relay with five and a farther sample lead. Another has the same Camp bundle, a farther Relay with one and a nearer sample lead. Reading an optional landmark reveals the useful retained lead; reaching it does not silently collect. The second layout should prompt a different route or return decision, not five compulsory identical stops.

This is one candidate, not approved balance or the complete field mechanic. Open movement retains honest water/tree/boulder blockers. Procedural generation needs different worthwhile opportunities and reachable discoveries, not only a fresh maze. Terrain does not reveal genes or promise the desired phenotype. Expedition kinds still need distinct authored purposes; naming three identical routes is insufficient.

## Worked genomic investigation

Today's two authored sample profiles support alternatives, not one hidden already-living individual. A can support plain coat with pale variation carried, or expressed pale markings. B can support steady movement with lower walking effort, or burst capability with baseline walking effort. B cannot supply an efficient-burst combination assembled from half of each alternative. Expression references use the same healthy/rested adult, mild, firm-ground conditions; no numerical speed or savings is claimed.

The proposed B question is: **“Do burst capability and lower walking effort occur together among this sample's supported forms?”** Two proposed operations answer different scopes:

| Operation | Sample A result | Sample B result | What changes next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Drive copy-range analysis** | One M copy shared across supported possibilities. | Zero or one M copy across supported possibilities; effort association unresolved. | A useful drive-copy range is established; the player can still investigate how effort belongs to the alternatives. |
| **Joint drive/efficiency analysis** | Shared `Mm/Ee`. | Paired supported `mm/Ee` and `Mm/ee`. | Both capability and effort pairing are established. Narrow drive inspection is free/redundant; heritage or coat may remain useful. |

These outputs need new authored evidence-to-fact mappings and changed study bundles; they are not current assays or proof that an experiment has been implemented. Grouping means “belongs to this eligible configuration,” not chromosome linkage. A sealed sample does not walk, and a reference animation does not measure a future creature.

The broader operation is honestly better when wanted and affordable. Different provisional resource classes can make narrower capability work useful when the broader analysis lacks supplies; no mandatory narrow prerequisite, artificial penalty or forced exclusivity should preserve a fake choice. Already established information remains free. Prices and overlap policy require owner steering.

The screen changes structurally: unanswered relationship → drive evidence with effort still unknown → two simultaneous supported pairings. Free directional focus explains each pairing in one anchored reference area. There is no guessed answer, gene installation, second paid reveal or result acknowledgement gate. This is a more concrete inquiry, but may still feel like purchasing an explanation; it is not yet an internally accepted active discovery game.

The existing two profiles will exhaust novelty quickly. A proof needs at least one contrasting authored case whose question and useful next operation differ. The genetic engine's larger set of valid combinations is not automatically eligible sample content. New body plans, genes, molecular chemistry, sample-to-genome distributions and future breeding are not silently selected here.

## Screen and physical-control pre-design

| Moment | Companion, 450×600 | Lab, 1024×600 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orient | Quiet mode/location; world and reached subject dominate; persistent cargo below. | Collection Overview describes all samples. Left destinations preview the focused sample immediately. |
| Investigate | Reached subject shows the actual Read/Take consequence. Different useful opportunities remain visible. | Main owns the exact question, authored evidence structure and remaining uncertainty; navigation contains destinations only. |
| Commit | One explicit collection or Send action; separate Lab acceptance. | Named analysis scope previews purpose and actual cost. One deliberate Start commits; free inspection requires no extra confirmation. |
| Learn | Retained lead stays useful after walking/reload. | Both supported records remain readable; focus updates the reference explanation without changing genes or granting another finding. Complete creation eligibility remains separate. |
| Continue | Choose the next useful subject or return. Acceptance clears current cargo and ends the outing. | Choose another useful uncertainty, resupply or inspect known results. Complete supported creation is a separate commitment. |

Directions move on a map, preview in collections and inspect within a workpiece. Horizontal arrangements use Left/Right, vertical ones Up/Down. Confirm enters a named task or commits its displayed action; it must not introduce an invisible review/Start chain. Back restores the same object and caller. No screen clicks, touch, added controls, preparation clocks or routine navigation footers.

Art direction inspected the actual C18 reference, original 450×600 Companion proof and delivered native partial-coat proof. The field should convey a place worth noticing; the bench should convey an inquiry becoming clearer. Use C18's established subject weight, graphite depth, defined blue framing and restrained warm focus. The current decorative activity emblem and tiny crops surrounded by prose do not satisfy that purpose. Proposed before/after evidence is a continuous workpiece, not a collection of web cards or a laboratory background. Unknown anatomy remains unknown; an attractive whole portrait cannot replace missing disclosure.

## Discussion boundary

Game design, UI/UX, genomics and art direction contributed researched critiques and exchanged concrete objections. They rejected hidden-single-genotype fiction, reference clips as assays, genotype receipts presented as active experimentation, fake narrow/broad choices and extra confirmation ceremony. This is proposal review, not visual production, playtesting or universal team acceptance of a game.

**Recommended next decision:** should research's core pleasure be choosing and interpreting meaningful analyses, or should the player operate a deeper fictional experiment? The worked B case gives that discussion a concrete starting point. If it still feels like a paid menu, design its investigative operation before commissioning art or coding another map iteration.

After direction, the smallest proof is a connected before/action/after paper storyboard for one field decision and one bench investigation, with exact knowledge transitions and controls. Architecture checks the selected scope/toolchains/framework boundaries before implementation. No new framework, environment, services, radio/hardware commitment or purchases follows from this packet.
