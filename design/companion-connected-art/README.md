## Meaning and production constraints

[Gameplay](../../specs/gameplay.md) owns the rules. Supplies are whole fungible items within class. Preparation and chance state are activity, never cargo or payment. The Probe DNA/net subject is fictional activity art, not a captured specimen, actual genome or sensor reading. Real route/state replace generic illustration captions.

Cargo browsing or cancelling an unsealed review preserves the outing. Send stops collection. At reception, incoming0/1/1 and Lab stock0/0/0 remain separate. Returning precedes acceptance. Fresh Accept stores once and ends the expedition; receipt closes metadata and the next outing has a new identity. Early ending does not award a completion sample.

Use one production family: physical blue Data card, gold Energy crystal, rounded translucent lime Essence. Sources differ in font treatment, pixel detail and resource shape; derive shared masters instead of cropping a different icon per state. Preserve graphite depth, slender defined stepped electric-blue frames, saturated subjects and one restrained warm focus. Remove repeated headings and technical prose. Existing physical Confirm/Back must drive distinct focus, pressed and committed feedback; remove baked control-letter decorations.
