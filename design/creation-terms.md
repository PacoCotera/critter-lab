# Creation terms: use the sample, keep the discovery

**Accepted V1 game design; not implemented.** The accepted research loop fully decodes the sample’s required genomic information before a complete supported configuration is selected. Creation introduces no hidden genetic lottery. These rules define what that commitment means for the player; exact prices, durations and resource identities remain unselected.

## Creation rule

**A researched sample supports one founder creation. Its findings remain in personal research records, with the conditions and versions that explain when they apply.** Spend the sample and the displayed creation supplies only when explicit creation is accepted. Keep its bitmap, findings, supported alternatives, selected configuration and creation history as an inspectable record afterward, clearly distinguished from unused material.

A discovered configuration is a reference plan, not an unlimited-use blueprint. Remembering a genome does not supply its material or establish that another sample supports it. This preserves a reason to investigate new samples while making learning durable rather than something repeatedly erased to sell the same experiment again.

## What the player keeps and spends

Before Create, review names the chosen complete configuration, sample, creation supplies and resulting ownership. The player can revise that choice or leave without spending creation inputs. Reagents already used in research remain spent: their accepted findings are retained.

At accepted Create, the chosen sample becomes used and the named supplies are spent together. The player gains the saved parentless founder; the sample remains its origin record, never a fictional parent. There is no additional payment to reveal, inspect, recover missing art or resume that creation. Research-only social references cannot replace the sample or provide donor genes.

Missing creation supplies prevent that commitment, not inspection or other eligible work. The Lab identifies what is missing and suggests collection or console preparation. Console-only play must provide qualifying samples and necessary supplies without owning a Probe or meeting another player. Requirements follow applicable processes and characteristics; ordinary biological culture is not mandatory for every critter.

## Creating again

To make another founder, obtain another qualifying sample through field collection or console investigation. Compare its evidence with saved findings. Familiar knowledge can suggest the useful test immediately. Accepted evidence applicable to this new sample under supported rules need not be purchased again; knowledge alone cannot copy measurements or findings from the earlier sample. However, the new sample must independently establish every required region and its supported complete configuration; visual similarity or a familiar label cannot mark it complete automatically.

Choosing another supported alternative from the used sample requires new qualifying material, not reopening its old result. That new material may support different alternatives. Compatible breeding is the other route to explore inheritance from actual parents, with its own consent and creation rules; this proposal supplies no automatic breeding permission or exact-copy guarantee.

This gives exploration, research and breeding different purposes. The cost is that the player cannot manufacture every alternative from one lucky find. Persistent comparison records soften that cost without turning discoveries into endlessly reusable stock. Cosmetic collection remains as valid a motive as functional specialization.

## A worked commitment and return

Mara fully researches a sample and selects one of its supported complete configurations. She lacks a creation ingredient, so she leaves the plan intact and prepares the ingredient at the Lab. On return she reviews the unchanged genome and commits Create.

Her sample is now used; the encyclopedia retains both the discovery and the alternative she did not choose. The saved individual belongs to Mara even if another household member is using the shared Lab when it becomes READY. Opening is deliberate. Later she can inspect or carry that same individual on the Companion, or pursue another sample to investigate the unchosen direction.

## Cancellation, interruption and failure

Before submission, Cancel leaves the creation plan and inventory intact. Once submitted, uncertain delivery means checking the same request before offering another commitment. A confirmed rejection spends no creation inputs. Once accepted, creation cannot be cancelled into fresh material or rerolled; leaving merely hides its progress.

Disconnection or failed visual production is not an in-world experiment failure. Retain the accepted individual and retry the same pinned creation job without further charges. If visuals cannot be produced, report them as unavailable rather than substituting a new individual. The remedy for permanent service or content failure still needs design; this proposal does not promise eventual recovery or an automatic refund. READY waits for Open; repeated Open reveals the same individual. This introduces no permanent-loss or neglect penalty. Crafting failures remain a separate, explicitly disclosed ingredient-loss/recovery mechanic.

**Still open:** exact prices, durations, content requirements, breeding rules and remedies for permanent service or content failure.
